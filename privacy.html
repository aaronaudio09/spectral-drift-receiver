Spectral Drift — Privacy policy

Applies to the Spectral Drift apps for Windows and Android, the web version, and the TV (Cast) receiver. Last updated October 25, 2026.

The short version

Spectral Drift draws visuals from sound. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you hear, see or set in the app is sent to the developer.

Sound

When you choose Mic, System or Device audio, the app analyses the sound on your device, in real time, only to draw the picture. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Pictures you load

An image you pick for the picture effects stays on your device. If you link your phone to your PC or cast to a TV, a small copy is sent to that PC or TV so it can show the same effect.

Settings

Your dials and choices are saved on your device. They are shared only with a PC or TV you link to yourself.

Phone remote

The phone remote talks to the Windows app directly over your local Wi‑Fi, after you enter the PIN the PC shows. These messages don't pass through the internet or any server.

Casting to a TV

When you cast, your TV loads the receiver page from GitHub Pages, and your settings are sent to the TV through Google Cast. GitHub and Google handle those connections under their own privacy policies (for example, they may log the TV's IP address).

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/aaronaudio09/spectral-drift-receiver.
